Lillith: bundle/models/
=======================

Put a GGUF chat model here (e.g. dolphin3.0-llama3.2-3b-q4_k_m.gguf) and
the setup wizard (Settings > Brain, or `lillith setup`) finds it first.
Your ~/AI/models folder is searched too.

This bundle ships no model weights. Nothing is downloaded: copy the
.gguf onto this computer yourself.
