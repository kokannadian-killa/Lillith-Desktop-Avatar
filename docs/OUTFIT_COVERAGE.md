# Outfit coverage

Seasonal outfits (`assets/seasons.toml`) are add-only: each one ships 4 clips of its own, and every other clip is
drawn in her base outfit. A manual pick (`seasonal_outfit = "<id>"`, right-click ▸ Avatar ▸ Outfit) stays on until Ko
changes it: across restarts, Show off, the date auto-pick, pose picks and config reloads. What it can not do yet is
change clips that have no art in that outfit.

## How an outfit reaches every animation

Add an **outfit take** of a clip: the same move, drawn in the outfit, as a normal manifest clip tagged
`seasonal`, `outfit:<id>`, `season:<id>` and `outfit_of:<clip>` (name it `<id>__<clip>`, e.g. `halloween__idle`).
While `<id>` is worn the animator draws it in place of `<clip>` everywhere: as the base loop, an idle take, a transition,
a reaction, a pose pick or Show off (`Animator::set_outfit`, `animator::OUTFIT_OF_TAG`). It is not a pose of its own (never
listed in the menu or picked by name), it is hidden while the outfit is off, and its own `jiggle_variant` is honoured.
Match the source clip's frame count, canvas (400x400, anchor 200,392) and timing so events and blends line up.

`catalog::clips_without_outfit_take(manifest, outfit)` lists the clips still missing a take (tested in
`lillith-anim`). Today no clip has one, so the list is the same for all six outfits
(halloween, xmas, newyear, valentines, spring, summer): 226 clips on main at 1184b90d.
